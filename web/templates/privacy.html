What we collect

DataWhy
Email and password (stored only as a salted hash)To create your account and sign you in
Name (optional)To greet you in the app
Age and languageTo recommend books readers of your age and language enjoy
Likes, dislikes and the books shown to youTo personalise recommendations and avoid repeating them
Your current chat with the advisorTo answer follow-up questions. Kept in server memory only, until you start a new chat or the server restarts
Sign-in sessions, account creation and last-activity times, the date you accepted this policyTo keep you signed in securely and to prove consent

Who else receives data

  • GigaChat (Sber) receives the messages you send to the advisor, with the matching books, to write the answer.
  • Google Translate receives your chat messages to translate them into English for the book search.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) receives only book titles and authors, to find a book's page. No personal data.
  • Google Fonts serves the app's font, so it sees your IP address when the page loads.

Don't put sensitive personal information in chat messages. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Sign-in sessions expire after 30 days. Aggregated, anonymous book statistics (for example, the total number of likes a book has) remain after deletion.

Your rights

  • See and correct your profile in the app's Profile tab.
  • Get a copy of your data: Profile → Download my data.
  • Withdraw consent and delete everything: Profile → Delete account. This removes your account, profile, reactions and history right away.
  • For anything else, write to the contact above.

Children

If you're under 16, or under the age of digital consent in your country, a parent or guardian must agree to this policy on your behalf.

Changes

If this policy changes, the app will ask you to review and accept the new version before you continue.
